## Abstract

PowerShell remoting is a very powerful tool, especially when your endpoints are constrained, and your credentials are not locally stored. In this session, I'm going to explore examples and use cases of that combo. We will also explain how to avoid common issues when working with such a setup, and how to take full advantage of this configuration.
